Marta

Marta is a private assistant for one family. It helps plan trips, books them in the family's own accounts once someone approves, and reminds each person what is on their day.

Marta is run by Oleksii Hrytsenko (Alex) and Frol Buzdalin and is not a travel agent. Nothing is booked without a person pressing a button to approve it.

To connect your Google Calendar, you first need an invitation from the family. Marta then asks Google for permission to read your calendar. It cannot change or delete events.
