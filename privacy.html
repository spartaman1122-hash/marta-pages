Marta: privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who we are

Marta is a private family assistant built and run by Oleksii Hrytsenko (Alex) and Frol Buzdalin, 5 Warwick Close, 355 Kensington High Street, London W8 6NP. Contact: alex.hry1122@gmail.com. We are not a travel agent and we do not sell travel. Marta helps a family plan trips and book them in the family's own accounts.

What Marta reads

If you connect your Google Calendar, Marta can read your events so it can tell you what is on your day and remind you before a meeting. It asks Google only for permission to read your calendar. Marta cannot create, change or delete events.

From a calendar event Marta uses the title, time, location, the video-call link and the names of the other people invited. Meeting descriptions are read only to help you prepare, and are not kept.

Marta also keeps what you tell it in chat: trip requests, your preferences and rules, and the messages between you and Marta. Some of that can be personal, including health information if you choose to share it.

How the calendar connection is kept

The access Google gives Marta is stored encrypted, is read only when Marta needs it, and is never shown to the AI model or written in a log. You can remove the connection at any time, either in Marta or in your Google Account under "Third-party apps with account access".

Why we use it

Only to send you the reminders and day plans you asked for. We do not use calendar contents for advertising, to train AI models, or for anything else.

Who else sees it

  • Supabase (London, UK/EU region) stores Marta's data.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model that reads your messages. Anthropic keeps the messages sent to it for 30 days, and may access them for safety and security purposes. It does not use them to train its models without permission.
  • Google provides the calendar. Its own privacy policy applies to what Google holds.
  • Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Calendar contents are not stored: Marta reads them when it needs them and keeps nothing. Chat history and trip records are kept for as long as the family uses Marta. You can ask us to delete your data at any time, and when the family stops using Marta it is deleted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, and to object to how it is used. Write to alex.hry1122@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in Marta before the change takes effect.
